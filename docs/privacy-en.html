Privacy Policy

RinkLogic · Effective: 23 October 2026

In short: RinkLogic has no in-app developer server, account system, advertising, or tracking. The app processes workout data on the Garmin device. Voluntary beta testers manually return the original FIT and reference form to the developer through a private transfer channel.

1. Data processed by the app

While you use RinkLogic, the app may process heart rate and heart-rate zones, selected Garmin profile values needed for zone calculations, accelerometer and gyroscope measurements, recording time, manually marked shift boundaries, and metrics derived from those inputs. Depending on device support, derived motion metrics may be unavailable.

2. Purpose and storage

The data is used only to record and display a hockey workout, including shifts, time on ice, heart-rate information, and supported derived metrics. RinkLogic writes workout and app-specific fields to a Garmin FIT activity file stored through Garmin's activity-recording system.

3. No automatic transmission; voluntary beta test

The app does not request a communications or internet permission and sends no data automatically to RinkLogic or its developer. It contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDK. Beta participants receive a random pseudonymous tester ID and may manually submit the original FIT, device/firmware details, and a reference form through a private upload-only or individually encrypted channel. These files include heart-rate, motion, timing, shift, and derived distance data. Real files must not be posted to public GitHub issues, public folders, or public chats.

4. Optional beta-test video

Video is not required and is processed only with separate, explicit consent. Consent for FIT data does not include video.

5. Garmin services

If you synchronize the activity, Garmin may transfer and process the FIT activity through Garmin Connect according to your Garmin settings and Garmin's own privacy terms. This processing is controlled by Garmin, not by ShiftSense Hockey. See the Garmin Connect Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Outside the voluntary beta test, the developer keeps no separate copy. Submitted pilot files are retained for no more than 90 days. Withdrawal stops further use; still-identifiable pilot data is deleted within seven calendar days after an applicable withdrawal request unless a legal duty requires otherwise. Device and Garmin Connect data remain separately controlled by the user and Garmin.

7. Permissions

  • FIT and FIT Contributor: create the activity and add hockey-specific fields.
  • Sensor: read heart-rate and supported motion-sensor data during recording.
  • User Profile: read values needed to calculate and display heart-rate zones.

8. Sharing and sale

The developer does not sell or rent data. Pseudonymous beta data is used only for the described pilot and reproducible local analysis and is not published.

9. Security and limitations

Processing is designed to remain within the Garmin device and Garmin's activity system. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their Garmin account and device and review their Garmin privacy settings.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the coachrichie GitHub profile.

RinkLogic is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Garmin.
