Privacy Policy

ShiftSense Hockey · Effective: 28 September 2026

In short: ShiftSense Hockey has no developer-operated server, account system, advertising, or analytics. The app processes workout data on the Garmin device and stores it in the activity file. The developer does not receive your workout data.

1. Data processed by the app

While you use ShiftSense Hockey, the app may process heart rate and heart-rate zones, selected Garmin profile values needed for zone calculations, accelerometer and gyroscope measurements, recording time, manually marked shift boundaries, and metrics derived from those inputs. Depending on device support, derived motion metrics may be unavailable.

2. Purpose and storage

The data is used only to record and display a hockey workout, including shifts, time on ice, heart-rate information, and supported derived metrics. ShiftSense Hockey writes workout and app-specific fields to a Garmin FIT activity file stored through Garmin's activity-recording system.

3. No direct transmission to the developer

The app does not request a communications or internet permission and does not send data to ShiftSense Hockey or its developer. It contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDK.

4. Garmin services

If you synchronize the activity, Garmin may transfer and process the FIT activity through Garmin Connect according to your Garmin settings and Garmin's own privacy terms. This processing is controlled by Garmin, not by ShiftSense Hockey. See the Garmin Connect Privacy Policy.

5. Retention and deletion

ShiftSense Hockey does not maintain a separate copy of your data. Retention and deletion of activity files on the device and in Garmin Connect are controlled by you and by Garmin's services. You can delete activities using the controls provided by the device or Garmin Connect.

6. Permissions

  • FIT and FIT Contributor: create the activity and add hockey-specific fields.
  • Sensor: read heart-rate and supported motion-sensor data during recording.
  • User Profile: read values needed to calculate and display heart-rate zones.

7. Sharing and sale

The developer does not sell, rent, or share personal data because the developer does not receive it through the app.

8. Security and limitations

Processing is designed to remain within the Garmin device and Garmin's activity system. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their Garmin account and device and review their Garmin privacy settings.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the coachrichie GitHub profile.

ShiftSense Hockey is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Garmin.
